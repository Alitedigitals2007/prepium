## What You Can Do on Prepium

### 1. Take Practice Tests (CBT Style)
- Pick your subjects, choose how many questions, set a timer
- Get your score + percentage + breakdown by subject immediately
- Review every question and see the correct answers
- First 2 tests are free. After that, you pay once and get unlimited access

### 2. Upload Any PDF Lecture Note → AI Turns It Into a Quiz
- Upload a PDF of your lecture note or handout
- AI reads it and creates practice questions from that exact material
- You choose: multiple choice, fill-in-the-blank, essay, or mixed
- You choose the difficulty: easy, medium, hard, expert
- Best for revising right before an exam using your own materials

### 3. Daily Challenge Arena
- 10 fresh general knowledge questions every day (math, science, English, history, logic, tech)
- Compete with students from other universities on the leaderboard
- Only one shot per day — keeps you coming back

### 4. Study Hub (Your Personal Study Room)
- **Create courses** (e.g., PHY 101, CHM 102) and organize topics
- **AI writes explanations** for any subtopic — like having a tutor explain it to you
- **Study with a timer** (Pomodoro-style) — the app tracks your total study time
- **AI tutor chat** — ask questions about what you're studying, get answers instantly
- **AI flashcards** — generate question-and-answer cards from any topic, flip through and practice
- **AI quizzes** — take practice tests on specific topics within your course
- **YouTube search** — the AI finds relevant YouTube videos for each subtopic
- Import courses straight from your school timetable
- Track your progress: total sessions, total minutes, streak, flashcards created

### 5. CGPA Calculator + Academic Tracker
- Set up your profile (department, level, 4.0 or 5.0 scale)
- Add all your courses with grades and credit units
- See your **real-time CGPA** and degree class (First Class, Second Class Upper/Lower, etc.)
- **What-if analysis**: "What if I retake this F and get an A?" — see exactly how your CGPA changes
- **AI prediction**: The AI looks at your grades and predicts your likely GPA range next semester, warns you about at-risk subjects, and gives study tips
- **AI academic advisor**: Chat with AI about your academic situation and get advice
- Export your full transcript as a structured report
- Hide courses you don't want in the calculation
- Carry forward CGPA from a previous school

### 6. School Timetable
- Add your courses with days, times, and venues
- **Upload a photo or PDF of your timetable** — AI automatically extracts everything
- Edit or delete slots anytime
- Always accessible from your phone

### 7. AI Reading Timetable (Personal Study Schedule)
- Tell the app how many hours per day you want to study and your preferred time window
- It **automatically creates a weekly study schedule** spreading sessions across the week
- Good study sessions and rest days are balanced automatically
- Regenerate as many times as you want

### 8. Deadline Tracker
- Add assignment and exam deadlines with date and time
- **Email reminder 1 hour before** the deadline
- See all deadlines at a glance

### 9. Daily Task Dashboard
- See your timetable, reading schedule, deadlines, and personal goals on one page
- Create goals and tick them off
- The app can **auto-generate your to-do list** for the day
- See your weekly summary: tasks done, study minutes, quiz scores

### 10. Upload Your Own Study Materials
- Upload PDFs, documents, images to your personal library
- View, download, or delete them anytime
- Each subject shows how many questions are available

### 11. Refer Friends & Earn Money
- You get your own referral code
- When someone you refer pays for premium, you earn **₦300 (15% commission)**
- Track your referrals, earnings, and balance on your dashboard
- Withdraw your earnings (min ₦100, 10% fee)

### 12. Other Good Stuff
- **History**: See all your past quiz attempts and scores
- **Profile**: View your stats — streak, CGPA, study minutes, quiz history
- **Login streak**: The app tracks how many days in a row you log in
- **Notifications**: Get announcements from the platform
- **Password reset**: Forgot your password? Reset it via email
- **Works on any phone**: Open in any browser, no app store needed
